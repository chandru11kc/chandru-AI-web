About SoleStride

The narrative of athletic propulsion, materials science, and uncompromising craftsmanship.

OUR MISSION

Defying Gravity One Stride at a Time

SoleStride was conceived in 2021 by a collective of biomechanical engineers, marathon runners, and master Italian cordwainers. Frustrated by commercial mass-market sneakers that sacrificed joint stability for flashy marketing, we established our own advanced prototyping facility in Brooklyn, New York.

Today, every model in our catalog—from our dual carbon-plate marathon racers to our hand-burnished Florentine brogues—undergoes rigorous kinetic testing to ensure optimal rebound, zero arch strain, and enduring aesthetic mastery.
